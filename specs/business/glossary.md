# Glossary

This glossary defines business terms that must be used consistently across the product, code, documentation, and AI agent tasks.

## User

A person using the mobile app.

## Account

The user's app identity.


## Authentication

The process of verifying the user's identity.

## Registration

The process of creating a new account.


## Login

The process of entering the app using existing account credentials.


## Logout

The process of ending the current authenticated session.


## Property

A real estate unit that can be evaluated as an investment opportunity.

A property may include price, size, area, expected rent, service charges, property type, completion status, and investment metrics.

## Area

A geographic investment zone such as Dubai Marina, JVC, Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, or Dubai Hills.

## Investment Opportunity

A property or area that may be attractive for investment based on available assumptions and metrics.

## Rental Yield

The annual rental income divided by the property purchase price.

Formula:

`Rental Yield = Annual Rent / Property Price * 100`

## Gross Rental Yield

Rental yield before deducting expenses.

## Net Rental Yield

Rental yield after deducting estimated annual costs.

Formula:

`Net Rental Yield = (Annual Rent - Annual Costs) / Property Price * 100`

## Annual Rent

The expected total rent generated by a property in one year.

## Monthly Rent

The expected rent generated by a property in one month.

## Property Price

The estimated purchase price of the property.

## Price Per Sqft

The property price divided by the property size in square feet.

Formula:

`Price Per Sqft = Property Price / Size Sqft`

## Service Charges

Recurring building or community maintenance fees.

For investment calculations, service charges are treated as annual costs unless otherwise specified.

## Annual Costs

Estimated yearly costs related to owning the property.

May include:

- Service charges
- Maintenance estimate
- Property management fee
- Vacancy estimate
- Other recurring ownership costs

## Cash Flow

The estimated money left after income and expenses.

For the demo version, cash flow can be calculated monthly or yearly.

## Monthly Cash Flow

Estimated monthly income minus estimated monthly expenses.

## ROI

Return on Investment.

In this app, ROI is a broader business metric than rental yield.

It may include rent, estimated appreciation, costs, and investment assumptions.

## Capital Appreciation

The estimated increase in property value over time.

## Appreciation Potential

A qualitative or numerical estimate of how much an area or property may increase in value.

## Occupancy

The expected percentage of time a property is rented.

## Vacancy

The expected percentage of time a property is not rented.

## Demand Level

A simplified estimate of market demand.

Allowed values:

- Low
- Medium
- High

## Risk Level

A simplified estimate of investment risk.

Allowed values:

- Low
- Medium
- High

## Investment Score

A normalized score from 0 to 100 that summarizes how attractive a property or area appears based on defined assumptions.

Higher score means the opportunity appears more attractive.

The score is not financial advice.

## Watchlist

A saved list of properties or areas the user wants to monitor.

## Comparison

A side-by-side evaluation of multiple properties or areas.

## Market Trend

A change in market metrics over time, such as price, rent, yield, demand, or transaction volume.

## Mock Data

Demo data created for product simulation.

Mock data must be realistic and internally consistent.

## Estimate

A calculated or assumed value that may not represent guaranteed real-world outcomes.

The app must clearly treat calculations as estimates.
