# Feature: Market Trends

## Purpose

Show users how market metrics are changing over time.

For the first demo, trend data may be mocked.

## User Value

Users can:

- Understand whether prices are rising or falling.
- See rental trend direction.
- Review demand movement.
- Compare areas over time.
- Make decisions using more than static numbers.

## Main User Actions

- View market trends.
- Filter trends by area if supported.
- Open area details from trend cards/charts.
- Review short AI trend insight.

## Displayed Information

Market trends may include:

- Price trend
- Rent trend
- Rental yield trend
- Demand trend
- Transaction volume trend
- Area-level movement
- Simple charts

## Business Rules

- Trend data may be mocked but must be realistic.
- Trend direction should be clearly labeled.
- Charts should not imply certainty.
- Trend insights should be short and grounded in visible data.
- The app should avoid overloading users with too many charts.

## Acceptance Criteria

- User can view at least one trend section.
- User can understand whether a metric is increasing, decreasing, or stable.
- Trend values are labeled clearly.
- Trend information does not claim guaranteed future performance.
