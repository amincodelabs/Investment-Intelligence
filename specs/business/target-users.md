# Target Users

## Primary User

Individual real estate investors who are interested in Dubai/UAE property investment.

They may have some understanding of investment concepts, but they are not necessarily professional analysts.

They care about:

- Purchase price
- Rental income
- Rental yield
- Cash flow
- Area demand
- Capital appreciation
- Risk level
- Liquidity
- Long-term value

## Secondary User

UAE expats who are considering buying their first investment property.

They may need a simpler explanation of investment metrics and risks.

They care about:

- Which area is safer to invest in
- Whether the expected rent makes sense
- Whether the property is overpriced
- How much monthly cost they may have
- What they should compare before buying

## Future Possible Users

These are not required for the first demo, but the product may later support:

- Real estate advisors
- Small investment groups
- Property analysts
- Mortgage advisors
- Real estate agencies
- Developers

## User Goals

Users want to:

- Find investment opportunities faster.
- Avoid poor investment decisions.
- Compare areas and properties clearly.
- Understand numbers without using spreadsheets.
- Track properties and areas over time.
- Get simple insights from complex market data.

## User Expectations

The user expects the app to be:

- Clear
- Fast
- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Data-driven
- Easy to scan
- Honest about uncertainty

## User Pain Points

Common user problems this app should address:

- Too many property listings and not enough investment analysis.
- Difficulty comparing properties objectively.
- Confusing real estate terminology.
- Lack of clarity around rental yield and net return.
- Hard to understand whether a property is overpriced.
- Hard to track interesting opportunities manually.
