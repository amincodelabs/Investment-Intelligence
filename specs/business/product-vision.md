# Product Vision

## Product Name

Working name: Real Estate Investment Intelligence App

The name may change later. The product concept should remain focused on real estate investment analysis.

## Vision

The app helps property investors quickly understand which real estate areas and properties may be attractive investment opportunities.

The app focuses on Dubai/UAE real estate in the demo version.

The user should be able to answer questions like:

- Which areas look attractive for investment?
- Which properties have better expected rental yield?
- How do two or more properties compare?
- What is the estimated cash flow of a property?
- What risks should I consider before investing?
- Which saved areas or properties should I keep monitoring?

## Product Positioning

The app is an investment intelligence tool, not a property listing marketplace.

It should feel closer to a financial dashboard than a traditional real estate app.

## Product Goals

The app should help users:

- Discover promising investment areas.
- Understand key market metrics.
- Evaluate individual property opportunities.
- Calculate expected investment outcomes.
- Compare opportunities side by side.
- Save interesting areas and properties.
- Receive useful insights based on available data.

## Non-Goals

The app should not:

- Act as a broker.
- Allow users to purchase property.
- Guarantee profit or investment performance.
- Provide legal, tax, or regulated financial advice.
- Replace professional investment consultation.
- Require real backend integration in the first demo version.

## Demo Goal

The demo should look and feel like a real product.

Even if the data is mocked, the experience should be realistic enough to demonstrate:

- Strong product thinking.
- Clear investment domain understanding.
- Clean mobile user experience.
- Well-structured business logic.
- A scalable foundation for future backend integration.
